Frequently asked questions

How do I change filters?

Swipe left or right on the preview, tap a filter in the strip below it, or tap the grid button to see every filter at once. Some filters, like Console '95, Mono LCD and ASCII, have a style option at the top of the screen.

The camera preview is black or says "Camera Unavailable".

Lo-Fi Lens needs camera access. Turn it on in Settings > Apps > Lo-Fi Lens > Camera, then reopen the app.

My videos have no sound.

Sound needs microphone access, which the app asks for when you first switch to Video. Turn it on in Settings > Apps > Lo-Fi Lens > Microphone.

My photos aren't being saved.

Lo-Fi Lens saves to your Photos library and needs permission to add photos. Turn it on in Settings > Apps > Lo-Fi Lens > Photos. A warning icon on the Photos button means the last save failed.

Where are my photos and videos?

In the Photos app. Tap the thumbnail button at the bottom left of Lo-Fi Lens to open it.

Does Lo-Fi Lens collect my data?

No. Everything happens on your device. See the privacy policy.
