Lo-Fi Lens Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 10, 2026

Lo-Fi Lens is a camera app that applies retro-style filters to your photos and videos. This policy explains what the app accesses and what happens to it.

The short version

Lo-Fi Lens does not collect, store, share or sell any personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your photos and videos are processed entirely on your device and are never uploaded. The only time the app goes online is when the App Store shows or processes the optional tips.

What the app accesses, and why

Camera. Used to show the live filtered preview and to take photos and videos. Camera frames are processed on your device and are never sent anywhere.

Microphone. Used only in Video mode, to record sound with your videos. The app asks for microphone access the first time you switch to Video. If you decline, videos are recorded without sound.

Photo library (add only). Used to save the photos and videos you take to your Photos library. The app can only add items; it cannot see, read or change anything already in your library.

You can change any of these permissions at any time in Settings > Apps > Lo-Fi Lens.

Data we collect

None. Photos and videos you take are saved only to your own Photos library. While a video is being recorded, it is briefly stored in the app's temporary storage on your device and then moved into your Photos library.

Third parties

Lo-Fi Lens does not include third-party SDKs, advertising, or analytics services. Purchases, including the optional in-app tips, are handled by Apple through the App Store, under Apple's privacy policy. Lo-Fi Lens never receives your payment details.

Children

Lo-Fi Lens does not collect any data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
